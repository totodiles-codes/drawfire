Every match starts with 60 seconds where the whole lobby draws the arena in orbit above the Earth. Ramps, platforms, walls, hoops, letters, anything. Then you fight on whatever got drawn.

1

Draw the map · 60 s

Three tools and a limited amount of ink. Every stroke becomes solid geometry the moment you let go, and the bots in your lobby are drawing at the same time, each with its own name-tagged cursor.

1Ribbon

Draw in the air. Flat lines become floors, slopes become ramps, loops become hoops.

2Ground

Paint walkable paths. Close a loop and it fills into a platform.

3Wall

Lines on the floor rise into cover. Loops become rings to hide in.

∞Everyone

The whole lobby draws at once: springs, bridges, castles, even giant letters.

2

Fight on it

The strokes turn white, the camera drops you in, and the arena your lobby just drew is the level. Falling off the map counts, so does knocking someone off it.

SPACEMove

Run up the ramp you drew, jump, double-jump, then hold Space to glide on your jet.

12Weapons

A scoped laser rifle and a plasma lobber with splash and knockback, so yes, you can rocket-jump.

↻Keep drawing

Every match is a new map. After a round, keep drawing on top of the same one.

How it's built

  • Vanilla JavaScript, three.js and three-mesh-bvh, bundled with Vite.
  • Strokes are swept into bands and extruded slabs, then merged into one BVH for capsule collision.
  • Bots plan shapes, draw them with their own cursors, then fight with ledge probes and landing prediction.
  • Every sound is synthesised with WebAudio. The Earth, clouds and atmosphere are procedural.

Run it yourself 

git clone https://github.com/totodiles-codes/drawfire.git
cd drawfire
npm install
npm run dev          # http://localhost:5173
npm test             # geometry, physics and bot tests
